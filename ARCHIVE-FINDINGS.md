## 4. COPY WE CAN USE NOW

### Artist statement (better than the art book's — use this one)
> I am a self-taught artist, fuelled by a passion for creating visual narratives. I love painting
> colourful and vivid pieces. I find joy in crafting visual stories through pixels and colors,
> inspired by my own personal experiences. The digital medium allows me to create with a spectrum
> of styles, experiment with endless possibilities, and seamlessly capture my thoughts.

### "The Method" — a whole About section, in her words
> I keep things simple. Most of my art is inspired by a vision I have had which I sketch out first.
> **Music has been a massive part of my creative journey and many of my paintings are inspired by a
> particular song or line that stood out to me.** Sometimes my ideas come straight from visual
> expressions from my diary.
>
> I don't limit myself to any specific medium and enjoy painting in various ways such as on canvas,
> collages, and digital sketching. Sometimes I love to draw on top of things that inspire me.
>
> Since I am most comfortable using acrylics, I apply the same techniques to my digital work. I
> apply the same paint strokes I would use on canvas to my digital art, and often use the same
> brushes I love in my studio.

**Her influences, named:** Van Gogh, David Hockney, Henri Matisse.

### "The Collection" — the strongest writing in the whole project
> I look at art as a way of personal expression. Pursuing bold colours and shying away from
> perfectionism. It allows my inner child to play and learn... **Art has a way of teaching me who I
> am and where I want to be.** My objective is not to create a flawless image, but rather serve as
> a form of release... Pursuing art has been an exercise in letting go. It has shown me that work
> can be fun, healing and can resonate with others.
